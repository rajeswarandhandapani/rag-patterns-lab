# On-call Triage Guide

Start with customer impact, scope, and the earliest abnormal signal. Use correlation and trace IDs to follow a request. Check recent deployments before restarting services. Assign an incident commander for severity one and two incidents.

Preserve evidence during investigation. Record the timeline in UTC, decisions, and command outputs. Mitigate first, then diagnose deeply. A post-incident review is due within five business days.
